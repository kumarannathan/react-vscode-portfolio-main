# About Me

Hi there! I’m **Kumaran Nathan**, a passionate software engineer and problem-solver with a love for creating impactful and innovative solutions. Whether it's building an intuitive user interface, optimizing backend systems, or diving deep into data, I thrive at the intersection of technology and creativity.



I’ve had the privilege of wearing many hats in my journey—from working in UI/UX design at **Wolverine Soft Studio**, where I bridged technical and creative disciplines, to contributing to open-source projects like **Aam Digital**, enhancing tools that empower NGOs to make a difference. Along the way, I’ve developed a keen eye for detail, a commitment to collaboration, and a knack for tackling complex challenges.

Currently, I’m channeling my skills into building exciting applications like a **Letterboxd-inspired album review platform** and experimenting with **AI integration** in mobile apps. My work reflects my belief in crafting software that is not just functional but also delightful to use.
